# Northstar Air Traveler Guide

## Carry-on and checked bags

Every traveler may bring one personal item and one carry-on bag. A carry-on
must fit in the overhead bin and may measure up to 22 × 14 × 9 inches. Checked
bags may weigh up to 50 pounds. Keep medicine, identification, keys, and other
essentials in your carry-on rather than a checked bag.

## Arriving and boarding

Arrive at the airport at least two hours before a domestic flight and three
hours before an international flight. Boarding begins 40 minutes before
departure. The boarding door closes 15 minutes before departure, even if the
aircraft is still at the gate.

## Changes and cancellations

Flexible fares can be changed without a fee. A standard fare has a $75 change
fee plus any difference in fare. Any fare can be cancelled for a full refund
within 24 hours of booking when the flight was booked at least seven days
before departure.

## Delays and severe weather

The Northstar Air app and the airport departure board are the sources of truth
for flight status. Weather conditions alone do not prove that a flight is
delayed or cancelled. If Northstar Air cancels a flight or delays arrival by
more than three hours, travelers may rebook on the next available Northstar
Air flight or request a refund, with no change fee.

## Help at the airport

Travelers who need wheelchair assistance should request it at least 48 hours
before departure. Unaccompanied minors and travelers with pets must check in
with an airport agent; they cannot complete check-in only through the app.
